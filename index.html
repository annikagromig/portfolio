experience

Jozu

Jozu is an AI/ML infrastructure company focused on making the model lifecycle secure, governed, and easy to manage. Helping teams handle everything from model versioning to deployment with the same rigor traditionally applied to software supply chains.

As founding designer, I led UX across the platform that help teams securely manage the AI/ML model lifecycle, from versioning and packaging to deployment. I worked closely with engineers and data scientists to turn complex technical concepts into products people can actually understand and use.

I set the UX vision and strategy, owned the work end-to-end from research through advocacy, and built the design systems the team still relies on today.

The product is now used by 25,000+ people that executed 440,000+ tasks, and the Jozu & KitOps marketing site has reached 2M+ visitors.

jozu.com

KitOps

KitOps is an open-source CNCF project, built by Jozu, that packages AI/ML models, datasets, and code into secure, portable artifacts.

I built the KitOps brand from scratch, including its logo, visual identity, and overall design language for a security-focused open-source packaging tool, and worked as the project's sole designer across product, brand, and community. That work helped the project grow to 1,400+ GitHub stars and 300,000+ downloads.

kitops.org

about

A little about me

I’m a Senior Product Designer from Germany, now based in Vancouver, BC. I moved to Canada with my family in 2021, drawn by the mountains, the outdoors, and the chance to live a little closer to nature.

I like good design, curious people, and solving problems that don’t have an obvious answer. I’m happiest when I can get stuck into something complex, make sense of it, and turn it into an experience that feels simple and natural.

What gets me excited

When I’m not designing, I’m usually running, swimming, exploring the backcountry, or finding a good excuse to get outside. I also like slowing things down with watercolor sketches and baking bread, usually sourdough (Dumbledough is still going strong).

I’m a bit of a space nerd too, so I’ll happily get distracted by a good space documentary or a late-night rabbit hole about something happening somewhere very, very far away. And I’ll get unnecessarily excited whenever Andy Weir announces a new novel. I love books.
