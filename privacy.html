Privacy notice

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This is the personal portfolio website of Annika Gromig. This page explains what data is collected when you visit it and how you can control that.

What is collected

If you accept analytics, I use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use this site. It records things like the pages you view, how long you stay, your approximate location, your device and browser type, and the website that referred you. Google Analytics does not store individual IP addresses.

I don't collect your name, email address, or other personal details through this site.

Cookies and local storage

  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_BKE88TFZR3): set only if you click Accept. They help Google Analytics tell separate visits apart.
  • Your consent choice (analytics-consent): saved in your browser's local storage so the banner doesn't reappear on every visit. It is not sent anywhere.

If you click Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

Legal basis

Analytics data is processed on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.

Your choices

You can change your mind at any time using , here or in the footer of any page. Declining also removes the analytics cookies from your browser. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Where data goes

Analytics data is processed by Google, which may transfer it to servers outside your country, including the United States. See Google's privacy policy for details. The retention period for analytics data follows the settings configured in Google Analytics.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when delivering the site, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
