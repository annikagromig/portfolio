Privacy notice

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This is the personal portfolio website of Annika Gromig. This page explains what data is collected when you visit it and how you can control that.

What is collected

This site uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how people find and use it. It records things like the pages you view, the website that referred you, your approximate location (country), and your browser, operating system and screen size. Umami does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not store your IP address.

I don't collect your name, email address, or other personal details through this site.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set any cookies and does not use your browser's local storage, so there is nothing to accept or decline.

Legal basis

Analytics data is processed on the basis of my legitimate interest in understanding how this site is used.

Your choices

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, no analytics data is recorded for your visit. You can also block analytics with a content blocker or privacy-focused browser.

Where data goes

Analytics data is processed by Umami Cloud, which may store it on servers outside your country. See Umami's privacy information for details.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when delivering the site, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email hi@annikagromig.com or reach out via LinkedIn.
